Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Contact: support@hammerbyte.co.in

Who we are

HammerByte (hammerbyte.co.in) provides business software, including content publishing tools that can post to social platforms such as Instagram on behalf of accounts you connect.

Data we collect

  • Account details you provide when using HammerByte (name, email, workspace data).
  • Platform credentials you choose to store (for example Instagram Graph API access tokens and Instagram professional account IDs).
  • Media files and captions you upload for publishing.
  • Technical logs needed to operate the service (timestamps, publish status, error messages).

How we use Meta / Instagram data

When you connect Instagram through Meta’s APIs, we use the access token and Instagram user id only to publish content you explicitly start in HammerByte (for example feed image posts via Instagram Content Publishing). We do not sell this data. We do not use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

  • Credentials and media are stored on our servers to provide the feature you requested.
  • We share data with Meta/Instagram only as required to perform the publish API calls you initiate.
  • We may use infrastructure providers to host the application and files.

Retention and deletion

You can remove stored Instagram tokens and related credentials from Automation Users in the HammerByte app. You may also email support@hammerbyte.co.in to request deletion of your account data. See also our User Data Deletion page.
