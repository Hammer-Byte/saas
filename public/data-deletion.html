User Data Deletion

How to delete data associated with HammerByte and Meta / Instagram connections.

In the app

  1. Sign in to HammerByte.
  2. Open Automation Users.
  3. Edit or delete the automation user that stores your Instagram access token / user id (and other platform credentials).

By email

Email support@hammerbyte.co.in from your account email with subject “Data deletion request”. Include your HammerByte login email and any Instagram username involved. We will delete or anonymize stored credentials and related publish records within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited logs for security or legal reasons.
